# Privacy Policy

**Positively Locked** was built from the ground up with a **Privacy-First** philosophy. We believe your mental well-being and daily reflections should remain completely private.

---

## Zero Data Collection

- **No Tracking**: The app does not contain any third-party SDKs, analytics, or trackers.
- **No Remote Servers**: There is no backend server. The app does not connect to the internet.
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email, device identifier, or any other personal data.

---

## Local Storage Only

- The app stores the category you select and the current affirmation text on the device.
- Those values are saved with `UserDefaults` in the App Group `group.com.positivelylocked.shared`, so the widget extension can read them.
- There is no account, no favorites list, and no custom profile. This data stays on the device.

---

## Permissions

Positively Locked does not request access to:
- ❌ Your Location
- ❌ Your Contacts
- ❌ Your Camera or Microphone
- ❌ Your Photo Library
- ❌ Your Network or Bluetooth

The app does not ask for permission to send notifications.
