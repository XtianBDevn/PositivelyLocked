# Privacy Policy

**Positively Locked** was built from the ground up with a **Privacy-First** philosophy. We believe your mental well-being and daily reflections should remain completely private.

---

## Zero Data Collection

- **No Tracking**: The app does not contain any third-party SDKs, analytics, or trackers.
- **No Remote Servers**: There is no backend server. The app does not connect to the internet.
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email, device identifier, or any other personal data.

---

## Local Storage Only

- All of your data — including your selected mindset category, your favorited affirmations, and custom settings — is stored directly on your physical device.
- This data is saved using iOS `UserDefaults` within a secured **App Group** sandbox, which allows the main application to securely share your selected category with the Lock Screen widget extension.
- This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to the cloud.

---

## Permissions

Positively Locked does not request access to:
- ❌ Your Location
- ❌ Your Contacts
- ❌ Your Camera or Microphone
- ❌ Your Photo Library
- ❌ Your Network or Bluetooth

The app only requests permission to send local notifications (if you choose to enable scheduled background updates), which are processed entirely on-device by iOS.
