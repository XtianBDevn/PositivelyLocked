## App Overview

**Positively Locked** is designed to do exactly one thing beautifully:

1. **Pick Up (Raise to Wake)**: You are greeted with a beautifully styled, high-contrast positive affirmation on your Lock Screen.
2. **Unlock (Celebration)**: As soon as your face/fingerprint unlocks the device, the app transitions into a fluid particle celebration animation accompanied by synchronized, gentle haptic pulses.

## Key Features

- **Lock Screen Widgets**: Rectangular, circular, and inline widgets (iOS 17+ WidgetKit).
- **Stunning Celebration**: Particle physics engine built in SwiftUI.
- **Haptic Engine integration**: Custom-timed physical feedback using `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator`.
- **Offline & Private**: Zero tracking, zero network calls, zero data collected.
- **Shared App Group storage**: Seamless, secure data sharing between the main app and widget extension.
